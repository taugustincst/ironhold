**Full description**:

**Ironhold is an offline tower defense strategy game: build towers along the road, upgrade them, and hold the gate.**

**Build and upgrade.** Five towers, each with three levels. The Archer is fast and hits air, the Cannon splashes packed roads, Frost slows everything it touches, Tesla chains lightning between enemies, and Flame sets attackers burning. Pick a targeting mode for each tower (first, last, strongest, weakest), or sell it and rebuild when the plan changes.

**Read the horde.** Seven kinds of enemy, from fast runners to armored knights and flying wisps and drakes your ground towers can't touch. Every tenth wave brings the Warlord, a boss with an escort.

**Three maps, 120 waves.** Greenfield Pass is a winding country road and a good place to learn. On Frostvale Fork two roads converge, so you split your defenses. The Ashlands Gauntlet is a long march through burned earth. Each map is a 40-wave campaign; beat one and keep going in Endless mode.

**Play it your way.** Call the next wave early for bonus gold, run the fight at 1×, 2× or 3× speed, and earn up to three stars on each map. Quit between waves and pick the run up where you left it.

• Greenfield Pass, all 40 waves, is free. One purchase opens Frostvale Fork, Ashlands Gauntlet and Endless mode, for good; nothing else to buy
• No ads, no accounts, no analytics
• Plays fully offline: the whole game is inside the app
• Progress, stars and in-progress runs are saved on this device only
