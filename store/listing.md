**Full description**:

The road is open, the horde is coming, and the hold is yours to defend.

Ironhold is a complete tower defense game: place towers along the road, upgrade them as the waves grow stronger, and don't let anything reach the gate. No ads interrupt a run, nothing is sold inside the game, and your progress never leaves your device.

THREE MAPS, 120 WAVES
• Greenfield Pass, Frostvale Fork — where two roads converge — and the Ashlands Gauntlet, unlocked one after another as you win
• A 40-wave campaign on every map, with a boss every 10 waves
• Beat a campaign and keep going in Endless mode
• Earn stars for clean defenses and carry your progress between sessions

FIVE TOWERS, THREE LEVELS EACH
• Archer — fast, cheap, reliable
• Cannon — splash damage for packed roads
• Frost — slows everything it touches
• Tesla — chain lightning that jumps between enemies
• Flame — sets attackers burning over time
• Every tower upgrades through 3 levels; choose targeting modes, or sell and refund when the plan changes

SEVEN KINDS OF TROUBLE
• 7 enemy types, including flying units your ground plan can't ignore and armored units that shrug off light fire
• Call the next wave early for a bonus, and run the fight at 1×, 2× or 3× speed

NO ADS. NO PURCHASES. NOTHING COLLECTED.
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no analytics
• No network calls during gameplay — the game downloads once, then plays offline
• Progress, stars and in-progress runs are saved on this device only
