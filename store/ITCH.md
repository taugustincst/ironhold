# itch.io page — Ironhold Tower Defense

A draft for a free web demo on itch.io, written for 1.2.0 (free install with a
one-time unlock). The web build plays Greenfield Pass, all 40 waves; Frostvale
Fork, Ashlands Gauntlet and Endless mode are the Google Play edition, so the
itch page works as a free demo that points to Play. Nothing here is published
yet: the page is created by hand, as below, and only after 1.2.0 is live on
Play.

The zip is `ironhold-itch.zip`, built with
`python3 itch_pack.py <repo> ironhold-itch.zip` (the marketing tools). It holds
`index.html` at its root plus the files the page loads, and nothing from the
Android, test or store side. In a browser the two other maps show as part of
the full game, and the unlock screen says it is unlocked in the Google Play
edition.

## Upload

1. https://itch.io/game/new
2. **Kind of project:** HTML. Upload `ironhold-itch.zip` and tick
   **This file will be played in the browser**.
3. **Embed options:** *Embed in page*; viewport **360 × 640** (portrait); tick
   **Mobile friendly** (orientation: *Portrait*) and **Fullscreen button**.
   SharedArrayBuffer: off (not used).
4. **Pricing:** *No payments* (free web demo). Never *Paid* here while the
   Google Play edition is the paid one.
5. **Visibility:** *Draft* until the page below is filled in and checked,
   then *Public*.

**Description** (itch takes Markdown)

    **The road is open, the horde is coming, and the hold is yours to defend.**

    Ironhold is a tower defense game: build towers along the road, upgrade
    them as the waves grow stronger, and don't let anything reach the gate.

    **Five towers, three levels each.** Archer, Cannon, Frost, Tesla and
    Flame. Pick a targeting mode for each (first, last, strongest, weakest),
    or sell it and rebuild when the plan changes.

    **Seven kinds of enemy,** from fast runners to armored knights and flying
    wisps and drakes that ground towers can't touch, and the Warlord and its
    escort every tenth wave.

    **Call waves early** for bonus gold, run the fight at 1×, 2× or 3× speed,
    and earn up to three stars.

    **This web version is Greenfield Pass, the full 40-wave campaign.**

    **The full game is on Google Play:** Frostvale Fork, where two roads
    converge, the Ashlands Gauntlet, and Endless mode, 120 campaign waves in
    all. https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.taugustincst.ironhold

    No ads, no accounts. Progress stays in your browser.
