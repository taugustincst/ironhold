How to play

  • Tap an empty tile to build a tower. Towers can't be placed on the road or on rocks and trees.
  • Tap a tower to upgrade it, sell it, or change what it targets.
  • Start the next wave early to earn bonus gold. Every enemy that reaches the gate costs a life.
  • Flying enemies can only be hit by Archer, Frost and Tesla towers.
  • Armored enemies shrug off small hits. Use Cannons and Teslas against them.
  • Survive all 40 waves to conquer a map, then keep going in Endless mode.
